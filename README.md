HyrunX，专为 HYROX 选手打造的备赛工具。

既是老手的比赛模拟训练场，也是新手的备赛入门导师。模拟完整比赛、跟随 8 周计划、追踪每一项 PB、预估完赛时间 —— 一个 App 全部搞定。

---

**比赛模拟器**
一键开始完整模拟赛：8 轮，每轮 1km 跑步后接一个功能性训练站。实时计时、圈数追踪、语音播报。每次训练后记录完赛时间和主观疲劳度。

**8 周训练计划**
基础期 → 强化期 → 专项期 → 减量期。自动生成完整周期课表，每天训练点打勾完成。

**动作库**
8 个官方训练站的动作要点、Open / Pro 重量分级、常见错误。动作前快速复习，避免受伤。

**完赛时间预估**
录入各单项 PB，自动估算完赛时间。五维雷达图一眼看出心肺、下肢、握力、上肢、核心哪项是短板。

**训练日志**
记录每一次训练 —— 完整赛、间歇、力量、耐力 —— 时长、RPE、备注。周进度环让训练量一目了然。

**PDF 导出**
一键导出干净的成绩单 PDF，赛后复盘或个人记录都方便。

HyrunX is the race-day simulator built for HYROX athletes.

Part practice tool for veterans, part training guide for first-timers. Simulate the full race, follow an 8-week plan, track every PB, and predict your finish time — all in one app.

---

**RACE SIMULATOR**
One-tap full race: 8 rounds of 1 km run followed by a functional workout station. Live timer, lap tracking, voice prompts. Log your finish time and RPE after every session.

**8-WEEK TRAINING PLAN**
Foundation → build → specific → taper. A complete periodized plan generated automatically. Check off each session as you go.

**MOVEMENT LIBRARY**
Form cues, Open/Pro loading standards, and common mistakes for all 8 official stations. Review before you train.

**FINISH TIME PREDICTION**
Log your single-session PBs and get an estimated finish time. A five-axis radar chart shows exactly where you stand — cardio, lower body, grip, upper body, core.

**TRAINING LOG**
Log every session — full race, intervals, strength, endurance — with duration, RPE, and notes. Weekly progress ring keeps your volume honest.

**PDF EXPORT**
Export a clean race summary as PDF for post-race review or your own records.

**WHO IT'S FOR**

- First-time HYROX athletes who don't know where to start
- Veterans tracking PBs and race-day progress
- Gym athletes practicing race-day pacing
